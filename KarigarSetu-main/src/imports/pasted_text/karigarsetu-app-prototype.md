Design a complete **mobile-first web application prototype** called **KarigarSetu** for the Smart India Hackathon project **SIH26090 — “AI-Driven Market Linkage and Smart Cataloguing for Marginalized Artisans.”**

The website must be designed specifically for **phone screens only**, using a frame approximately **402 × 874 px**. Do NOT create desktop or tablet layouts.

## PRODUCT CONCEPT

KarigarSetu is an AI-powered digital marketplace that helps marginalized Indian artisans digitally catalogue their handmade products and connect with customers.

The core idea is:

**Artisan takes/uploads a product photo → AI identifies and catalogues the product → artisan reviews and approves the generated listing → product is published → buyers discover products through normal search or AI-powered recommendations → buyer places an order.**

The interface should feel like a real polished startup product that could be presented at the SIH preliminary round.

## DESIGN STYLE

Create a modern, premium, trustworthy interface inspired by Indian handicrafts without looking old-fashioned or overly decorative.

Use:

* Warm cream background: #FFF8EF
* Terracotta primary accent: #C65D3A
* Deep brown text: #2B211D
* Muted green secondary accent: #667A5B
* White cards: #FFFFFF
* Soft beige borders
* Subtle shadows
* Rounded corners
* Generous spacing
* Large, easy-to-tap buttons
* High readability
* Accessible contrast

Use **DM Serif Display** or a similar elegant serif font for major headings and **Inter** or a similar clean sans-serif font for body text and UI elements.

Use authentic-looking imagery of Indian artisans, traditional handicrafts, pottery, weaving, bamboo crafts, textiles, jewellery and handmade products.

Avoid excessive patterns, excessive gradients, neon colors, overly complicated animations, or generic corporate SaaS styling.

Choose Buyer → Marketplace → AI Search → AI Recommendations → Product Details → Cart → Checkout → Order Success → Order Tracking

Make buttons and navigation elements clickable in prototype mode.

## UX PRINCIPLES

The target artisans may have limited digital literacy.

Therefore:

* Minimize typing
* Prefer photo upload
* Use clear icons
* Use simple language
* Use large buttons
* Avoid complicated menus
* Provide clear feedback after actions
* Make AI assistance obvious
* Always allow the artisan to review and edit AI-generated information
* Keep important actions visually prominent

The overall product should communicate:

**Traditional Craft + AI + Digital Market Access + Human Empowerment**

The final prototype should look polished enough for an **SIH preliminary-round presentation**, with a clear end-to-end story from artisan product creation to customer purchase.
